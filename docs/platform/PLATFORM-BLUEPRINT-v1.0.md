# THINKamigBOB Platform Blueprint v1.0

Status: Approved and locked  
Phase: Phase 0 — Platform Architecture  
Purpose: Permanent architectural reference for platform development

## 1. Core Mission

THINKamigBOB exists to help the STEM teacher feel like they have an assistant while ensuring every student feels that their work is important, noticed, and worth continuing.

Everything in THINKamigBOB exists to showcase student growth—not the software itself.

## 2. Core Product Principles

1. Students are never blocked because teacher review is pending.
2. Students can always continue current work, return to previous work, and begin another available activity.
3. Students must not be able to determine whether the teacher has personally reviewed their work.
4. The platform may celebrate objective progress, but it must never falsely imply teacher review.
5. The platform must reduce teacher workload rather than create additional repetitive tasks.
6. Information already known by the platform or collected through an approved Google Workspace tool must not be requested again.
7. Student dignity and privacy must be protected.
8. No public rankings, leaderboards, or public red/yellow/green support labels.
9. Teacher judgment remains required where human judgment matters.
10. Chromebook usability is a primary platform requirement.

## 3. Classroom Rhythm

The permanent classroom rhythm is:

**Wins • Blockers • Next Steps**

### Beginning of class

- Yesterday’s Wins
- Class-wide Blockers
- Today’s Next Steps
- Current mission
- Optional teacher memo or agenda
- Lesson timer

### During class

- Progress tracking
- Teacher support signals
- Classroom jobs
- Kit checkout
- Teacher Feed
- Teacher Moments
- Lesson-stage countdown

### End of class

- Today’s Wins
- Remaining class-wide Blockers
- Tomorrow’s Next Steps
- Cleanup result
- Kit-return reminder
- What I Learned Today reminder

Student-facing Blockers must be class-wide and anonymous. Teacher-facing Blockers may identify individual students privately.

## 4. Student Reflection

The “What I Learned Today” Google Form is the only student reflection source.

The platform must not create a duplicate reflection form or reflection editor.

Builder, Workshop, Google Slides, and Google Vids provide evidence of work, not reflection.

## 5. Student Experience

Students enter through:

```text
Class code
→ roster-name selection
→ private identifier
→ Student Dashboard
```

Students do not require personal email accounts.

The Student Dashboard will eventually provide:

- Continue Current Mission
- Start or open available activities
- My STEM Work
- STEM Builder
- STEM Workshop
- Google Slides portfolio
- Google Vids portfolio
- What I Learned Today
- Classroom job
- Kit Manager controls when assigned
- Engineering Credits
- Badges
- Help
- Student profile

## 6. Teacher Experience

Teachers use email and password authentication, including email verification and password recovery.

The Teacher Command Center will eventually include:

- Today
- My Classes
- Students

- Progress
- Needs Attention
- Teacher Feed
- Teacher Moments
- Wins • Blockers • Next Steps
- Peer Helpers
- Classroom Jobs
- Kit Management
- Cleanup Challenge

- Portfolios
- Hall of Fame
- Reports
- Settings
- Smart Board Command Board

## 7. Student Progress

The teacher should eventually be able to see:

- Current activity
- Last-opened date and time
- Session count
- Page-open time
- Active work time
- Inactive time
- Evidence added, such as videos, images, slides, saves, or revisions
- Progress since the teacher’s previous check
- What I Learned Today submission status and response data
- Help requests
- Support signal
- Classroom jobs
- Helping history
- Kit-management history
- Engineering Credits
- Portfolio links

Activity time is an engagement clue, not a grade or proof of learning.

## 8. Support Signals

Teacher-only support signals:

- Green: progressing independently
- Yellow: check-in suggested
- Red: direct support suggested

Signals may use activity, evidence, help requests, inactivity, and Google Form reflection data.

The teacher can always override a suggested signal.

Students must never see their support classification.

## 9. Peer Helpers

Peer Helper status is:

- Teacher-controlled
- Skill-specific
- Separate from academic progress
- Never automatically assigned
- Not publicly displayed as a ranking

Examples:

- Builder controls
- Google Slides
- Google Vids
- Scale drawings
- Micro:bit setup

Helping credits require teacher control.

## 10. Classroom Jobs

Teachers can:

- Create custom classroom jobs
- Reuse jobs
- Assign directly
- Assign randomly
- Use fair rotations
- Exclude absent or ineligible students
- Preview random assignments before confirming

Students may earn Engineering Credits for completed jobs after teacher confirmation.

## 11. STEM Kit Checkout

Teachers can create numbered kits.

A student assigned as Kit Manager can record:

- Kit number
- Student receiving the kit
- Checkout
- Return
- Returned in good condition
- Needs teacher check

Kit controls appear only while the student is assigned that job.

## 12. Cleanup Challenge

Cleanup Challenge is an optional whole-class game mode.

- Classes earn collective cleanup credits.
- One class may be fairly selected as the Challenge Class.
- A great cleanup doubles earned cleanup credits and avoids a demerit.
- Missing the agreed cleanup standard may add one demerit.
- At the chosen threshold, such as ten demerits, a playful Fresh Start event occurs.
- Consequences must never humiliate students or remove meaningful instruction.
- Good sportsmanship is required.

## 13. Engineering Credits

Three distinct concepts must remain separate:

- Individual Engineering Credits
- Class Credits
- Class demerits

Every credit transaction records:

- Recipient
- Amount
- Reason
- Source
- Teacher or system origin
- Date and time
- Optional related mission, activity, job, or event

## 14. Positive Attention

The platform should create authentic positive attention through:

- Progress celebrations
- Teacher Moments
- Suggested shoutouts
- Wins
- Hall of Fame candidates

The platform may celebrate verified events such as:

- New evidence
- A saved design
- A revision
- Persistence
- Helping
- Leadership
- Kit stewardship
- Job completion
- Cleanup success

It must not claim the teacher reviewed work unless that event actually occurred.

## 15. Hall of Fame

The teacher controls all public recognition.

Possible categories:

- Engineering
- Innovation
- Teamwork
- Growth
- Persistence
- Stewardship

Hall of Fame recognition must provide multiple paths for students to be celebrated.

## 16. Smart Board Command Board

The Smart Board may eventually display:

- Greeting
- Current mission
- Optional teacher memo or agenda
- Total class-time countdown
- Current lesson-stage countdown
- Upcoming lesson stage
- Wins
- Class-wide Blockers
- Next Steps
- Shoutouts
- Friendly reminders
- Cleanup Challenge
- Class Credits
- End-of-class summary

The teacher may start, pause, resume, add time, reduce time, skip stages, return to a stage, or end class.

Timer changes never lock students out of their work.

## 17. Platform Intelligence Layer

The Platform Intelligence Layer organizes information; it does not replace teacher judgment.

It may:

- Build the Teacher Feed
- Group meaningful events
- Suggest Teacher Moments
- Suggest shoutouts
- Surface trends and blockers
- Prepare class summaries
- Prepare end-of-day briefs
- Recommend support priorities

It must not:

- Grade creativity automatically
- Publish student work automatically
- Assign Peer Helper status automatically
- Pretend teacher review occurred
- Use support signals as grades

## 18. Long-Term Expansion

After the core STEM platform is operational:

1. Explore a subject-neutral classroom platform where teachers in other content areas connect Google Drive resources and assignments to the same dashboard structure.
2. Explore a Challenge Studio where teachers and students create game-based question sets with automatically scored:
   - Multiple choice
   - Multiple select
   - True/false
   - Make It True
   - Whole-class play
   - Independent play

These are deferred roadmap items and are not part of PB-001.
